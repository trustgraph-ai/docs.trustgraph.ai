# Open source

TrustGraph is fully open source under a permissive licence. This is a
deliberate choice, not a limitation.

## What this means in practice

**No proprietary components.** The entire platform — every service,
every API, every processing pipeline — is open source. There are no
closed-source modules that unlock critical functionality. What you see
in the repository is what runs in production.

**Self-host anywhere.** Run TrustGraph on your own infrastructure —
on-premise, in your cloud account, or on a laptop. Your data stays
where you put it. There's no call-home, no telemetry, no dependency
on external services (beyond your chosen LLM provider).

**Full auditability.** You can inspect every line of code, every
processing step, every data flow. In regulated environments where you
need to demonstrate how AI systems work, this matters. You're not
trusting a vendor's claims — you're reading the source.

**No vendor lock-in.** TrustGraph works with multiple graph stores,
vector databases, LLM providers, and messaging fabrics. If you want to
swap a component, you can. If you want to fork and customise, you can.

**Community-driven development.** The roadmap, issue tracking, and
development process are public. Features are driven by real-world use
cases, not sales targets.

## TrustGraph Enterprise

For organisations that need commercial support, TrustGraph Enterprise
provides additional capabilities built on top of the open source
platform:

- Enterprise RBAC with fine-grained access control
- Attestation engine for cryptographic proof of AI reasoning
- Long-term support and SLAs
- Professional services and training

The open source platform is production-ready on its own. Enterprise
adds the governance, compliance, and support layer that large
organisations require.
